Farming

From Garden to Market

Before food reaches a market, a farmer has already invested time, effort and patience into producing it.

Preparing the land, planting seeds, caring for crops and harvesting are all important parts of the journey. The work may begin months before a customer ever sees the produce at the market.

🌱 The Lesson

Good results often come from many small efforts repeated with patience and care.

💭 Reflection

What are you working on today that could become something valuable in the future?

Why This Lesson Matters

Farming connects people, land and food. Farmers help provide fresh produce for families, communities, markets and businesses.

The journey from garden to market also teaches us that success does not usually happen in one moment. It is built through preparation, consistent work, problem-solving and patience.

When farmers produce food for their communities, their work can create income while also helping strengthen local food systems.
