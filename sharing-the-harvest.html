Kindness

Sharing the Harvest

A good harvest is a reward for hard work. But for many people, the joy becomes greater when they share what they have with family, friends and neighbours.

Sharing does not always require having a lot. Even a small gift can make a difference when it is given with kindness and a willing heart.

🌾 Moral Lesson

What we have can become more valuable when we use it to help and encourage others.

💭 Reflection

What is something you have today that you could share with someone else?

Why This Lesson Matters

Kindness strengthens relationships and helps communities support one another. Sharing food, knowledge, time or skills can create a culture where people look after each other.
