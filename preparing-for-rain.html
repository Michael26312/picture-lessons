Responsibility

Preparing for Rain

Rain can arrive when people least expect it. Preparing before it comes can make a difficult situation much easier to handle.

Simple preparation might include protecting important belongings, checking a roof, clearing drainage areas, storing supplies or making sure the family has what it needs.

🌧️ The Lesson

Preparing today can help us handle tomorrow's challenges with greater confidence.

💭 Reflection

What is one problem you can prepare for today instead of waiting until it happens?

Why This Lesson Matters

Responsibility includes thinking ahead and taking reasonable action before problems become emergencies.

Preparation does not mean that we can predict everything. It means using the information and resources available to us to reduce avoidable difficulties.

Planning ahead can help families, communities and businesses protect what matters and respond more effectively when unexpected situations occur.
