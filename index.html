About Picture Lessons

Picture Lessons is a visual storytelling project built around a simple idea: Every Picture Has a Lesson.

A farmer, parent, teacher, child, fisherman or community can show us lessons about perseverance, responsibility, kindness, patience, culture and hope.

Our goal is to connect meaningful photographs with stories and reflections that people can learn from and share.
