Farming • Hard Work

The Work Behind the Harvest

A harvest does not happen in one day. Farmers prepare the land, plant the seeds, care for the crops and wait patiently.

🌱 Moral Lesson: Hard Work

Good results often come from small efforts repeated every day. Never underestimate the value of patience and consistent work.

💭 Reflection: What are you working on today that may produce results tomorrow?
