About Picture Lessons

Picture Lessons is a visual storytelling project created to show that ordinary moments can contain extraordinary lessons.

A farmer, a parent, a teacher, a child, a fisherman or a community working together can teach us about perseverance, responsibility, kindness, patience and hope.

Our goal is to connect powerful photographs with meaningful stories and reflections that people can learn from and share.
