About Picture Lessons

Picture Lessons is a visual storytelling website built around one simple idea: Every Picture Has a Lesson.

Our lessons explore everyday experiences and the values behind them, including patience, education, family, farming, kindness, culture, responsibility, courage and community.

The goal is to make learning simple, memorable and accessible to people of different ages and backgrounds.
