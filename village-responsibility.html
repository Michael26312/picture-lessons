Leadership

Our Village, Our Responsibility

A community is more than the houses and land where people live. It is made up of people who share responsibilities, resources, traditions and a common future.

Good leadership begins with responsibility. Leaders and community members can work together to keep their surroundings safe, support young people, protect shared resources and solve problems peacefully.

🏘️ Moral Lesson

A better community begins when people take responsibility for the place they call home.

💭 Reflection

What is one problem in your community that you could help improve?

Why This Lesson Matters

Communities depend on participation. When people contribute their time, skills and ideas, they can help create cleaner, safer and stronger places to live.
