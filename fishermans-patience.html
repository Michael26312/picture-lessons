Patience

The Fisherman's Patience

A fisherman can spend hours on the water without knowing exactly when the next catch will come. He prepares his equipment, watches the water and waits for the right moment.

Leaving too early may mean missing an opportunity. Giving up after a short wait may also mean losing the reward that was just around the corner.

🎣 Moral Lesson

Patience and persistence can help us recognize opportunities that require time to appear.

💭 Reflection

What are you waiting for that may require a little more patience?

Why This Lesson Matters

Life does not always give us immediate results. Sometimes success requires preparation, persistence and the willingness to keep going while we wait for the right opportunity.
