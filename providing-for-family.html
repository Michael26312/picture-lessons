Responsibility

Providing for the Family

Providing for a family involves more than earning money. It can include preparing food, caring for children, sharing responsibilities and supporting one another through difficult times.

Different members of a family may contribute in different ways. Work, knowledge, household skills, encouragement and care can all help a family remain strong.

❤️ The Lesson

Responsibility means doing what we reasonably can to care for the people and responsibilities entrusted to us.

💭 Reflection

What is one useful thing you can do today to support your family?

Why This Lesson Matters

Families often depend on shared effort. One person may earn income while another manages the home, cares for children, grows food or provides practical support.

Recognising different contributions helps us understand that supporting a family is a shared responsibility rather than the work of only one person.

Small acts of care and responsibility can make a meaningful difference. When family members support each other, they can face challenges with greater cooperation and resilience.
