Leadership Lessons

Discover meaningful lessons about leadership, responsibility, service and helping people work together toward a better future.

What Is Leadership?

Leadership is not only about having a title or position. Leadership can mean taking responsibility, helping others, making thoughtful decisions and setting a positive example.

Strong communities are built when people are willing to contribute, listen to others and take responsibility for the things they can improve.

Our Village, Our Responsibility

A strong community depends on people who are willing to take responsibility and help solve problems together. This lesson explores leadership through service and community responsibility.

Read the Lesson

Lessons We Can Learn From Leadership

  • Leadership begins with responsibility.
  • Good leaders listen to the people around them.
  • Serving others can be an important form of leadership.
  • Leading by example can influence positive behaviour.
  • Communities become stronger when people work together.
