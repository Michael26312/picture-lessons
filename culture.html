Culture Lessons

Culture carries knowledge, traditions, stories and values from one generation to another.

Learning from elders and respecting traditional knowledge can help communities understand their history while passing important lessons into the future.

Older generations can carry knowledge built through many years of experience. Their stories, observations and practical knowledge can provide valuable lessons for younger people.

Why Culture Matters

Culture can connect people with their families, communities and history. Language, stories, traditions, skills and knowledge can all contribute to a community's identity.

Respecting culture does not mean refusing to learn new things. It can mean understanding where knowledge comes from and valuing lessons that have been passed between generations.
