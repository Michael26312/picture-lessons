Caring for the Land

The land provides people with food, water, shelter and many other resources. Forests, gardens, rivers and oceans are part of the environment that communities depend on.

Taking care of the land means using natural resources responsibly, reducing waste and protecting places that future generations will also need.

🌿 Moral Lesson

We should care for the land we depend on and leave it healthy for the people who come after us.

💭 Reflection

What is one small action you can take today to protect the environment?

Why This Lesson Matters

A healthy environment supports communities and future generations. Simple actions such as planting trees, keeping waterways clean and avoiding unnecessary waste can contribute to a healthier environment.
