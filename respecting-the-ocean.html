Respecting the Ocean

The ocean provides food, transportation, recreation and livelihoods for millions of people. For coastal communities, the sea can be closely connected to everyday life.

Keeping the ocean clean and using marine resources responsibly helps protect the environment that people and wildlife depend on.

🌊 The Lesson

When we care for nature, we help protect the resources and environment that future generations will depend on.

💭 Reflection

What is one simple action you can take to help keep your local environment clean?

Why This Lesson Matters

Healthy oceans support marine life and provide important resources for coastal communities. Pollution and careless use of natural resources can damage these environments.

Respecting the ocean can begin with simple actions such as reducing litter, disposing of waste properly and avoiding activities that unnecessarily damage marine habitats.

Caring for nature is a shared responsibility. The choices people make today can influence the environment that communities and future generations experience tomorrow.
