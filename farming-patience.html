Farming

The Farmer's Patience

A farmer plants today knowing that the crop will not be ready tomorrow. The work requires patience, care and faith in the future.

Every stage matters. The land must be prepared, seeds must be planted, crops must be cared for and the farmer must wait for the right time to harvest.

🌱 Moral Lesson

The best results often require patience before the reward arrives.

💭 Reflection

What are you building today for tomorrow?

Why This Lesson Matters

In farming and in life, people often want immediate results. But meaningful growth usually takes time. A farmer teaches us that consistent effort and patience can eventually produce a harvest.
